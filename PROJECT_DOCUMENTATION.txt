1. EXECUTIVE PROJECT OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SwarX is an enterprise-grade cyber-forensic platform designed to detect 
AI-synthesized voice clones, commercial Text-to-Speech (TTS), and physical replay 
attacks in real-time, while establishing an immutable, tamper-evident SHA-256 
Blockchain Audit Ledger for every forensic scan.

The Core Problem Solved:
* Generative Voice Clones (ElevenLabs, RVC, VALL-E): Highly realistic vocal 
  duplication used in CEO fraud, banking authorization bypass, and social 
  engineering.
* Commercial Neural TTS (Google Cloud, Amazon Polly, OpenAI): Clean synthetic 
  speech designed to fool human listeners and standard classifiers.
* Acoustic Spoofing & Replay (ASVspoof Attacks): Recorded human audio played 
  back through loudspeakers to spoof biometric voiceprint systems.
* Chain-of-Custody & Auditability: Need for cryptographically verifiable, 
  non-repudiable proof that an audio file was scanned, timestamped, and unaltered.

5. DETAILED SYSTEM DESIGN & SUBMODULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.1. Audio Feature Extraction Pipeline (ml-model/src/features.py)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Transforms raw audio into a 147-dimensional forensic feature vector:
1. MFCCs + 1st/2nd Order Derivatives (60 dims): Formant transitions and 
   spectral envelope trajectories.
2. Vectorized Autocorrelation Pitch Tracker: Evaluates fundamental frequency (F0)
   across 65 Hz - 500 Hz in ~15 ms and calculates cycle-to-cycle micro-jitter:
     Jitter = [ sum(|F0(t+1) - F0(t)|) / (N-1) ] / mean(F0)
3. Spectral Flatness (2 dims): Quantifies harmonic peak structure vs neural 
   vocoder smoothing.
4. 7-Band Spectral Contrast (14 dims): Formant peak-to-valley energy ratio 
   across 7 octave sub-bands.
5. Spectral Rolloff (2 dims): High-frequency energy cutoff (commercial TTS 
   typically cuts off sharply at 2.8 kHz - 3.2 kHz).
6. Zero-Crossing Rate & RMS Energy (4 dims): Organic breath dynamics and 
   amplitude decay contours.

5.2. Multi-Factor AI Inference Engine (ml-model/src/predict.py)
---------------------------------------------------------------
Fuses three detection signals:
* ASVspoof + Commercial TTS Trained Classifier (Random Forest + Gradient Boosting)
* Pretrained Wav2Vec2 Deep Transformer (MelodyMachine/Deepfake-audio-detection-V2)
* Physical Acoustic Telemetry (Micro-Jitter, Spectral Rolloff, Flatness)

Decision Fusion Matrix:
- If strongest fake signal >= 0.50:
    Combined Risk = (MaxFake * 0.60) + (MLScore * 0.20) + (AcousticScore * 0.20)
- If strongest fake signal <= 0.25 (High-Confidence Authentic Human Voice):
    Combined Risk = (MLScore * 0.45) + (NeuralScore * 0.25) + (AcousticScore * 0.30)
- Intermediate / Borderline:
    Combined Risk = (MLScore * 0.40) + (AcousticScore * 0.35) + (NeuralScore * 0.25)

5.3. Cryptographic Blockchain Audit Ledger (backend/services/ledger_service.py)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every audio scan is permanently minted into an immutable, chained JSON ledger:
* Block Payload includes:
    - Block Index & Timestamp
    - Audio SHA-256 Checksum
    - Filename & File Size
    - Verdict, Threat Tier & Risk Score (0-100%)
    - Previous Block Hash (prev_hash)
    - Current Block Hash (block_hash = SHA-256(index + prev_hash + timestamp + ...))
* Cryptographic Integrity Verification:
  Recalculates sequential hashes starting from Genesis Block #0. Any data tampering 
  immediately flags the ledger status as COMPROMISED.

5.4. Frontend Presentation & Dashboard (frontend/src/App.jsx)
-------------------------------------------------------------
* Dual-Tab Ingestion: Audio file uploader (.wav, .mp3, .flac) + Live studio microphone
  recording using native MediaRecorder with real-time waveform oscillations.
* Dynamic SVG Radial Risk Gauge: Smooth animated threat meter with color gradients
  (Green = Safe, Amber = Suspicious, Red = AI Voice Clone).
* Acoustic Telemetry Cards: Displaying Pitch Micro-Jitter, Spectral Rolloff, 
  Flatness, and Energy RMS.
* Blockchain Explorer: Live inspector showing cryptographic block hashes, payload 
  signatures, and verification badges.
* Scan History: Filterable historical table with one-click audit clearing.
